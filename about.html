About Fluenta

We think fluency is a habit, not a hurdle.

Fluenta started as a side project between three friends who kept abandoning their own language apps. Today it's a daily habit for 12 million learners in 42 languages — and we're still obsessed with the same question: what actually makes a lesson stick?

Our Story

Built by people who kept quitting the apps they built

Fluenta began in 2019 in a Hyderabad co-working space, after our founders realized their own streaks kept breaking the moment lessons stopped feeling like real conversation. So they scrapped the gamified drilling and rebuilt the product around one idea: practice should feel like talking to a person, because eventually, it needs to be.

Six years later, that same idea shapes everything we ship — from adaptive daily lessons to live conversation rooms hosted by native speakers around the world.

What We Believe

The principles behind every lesson

These are the calls we make when a feature idea sounds fun but doesn't actually help someone speak better.

🗣️

Speaking beats scoring

A perfect quiz streak means nothing if you freeze up in a real conversation. We design for the second one.

🔁

Consistency over intensity

Five honest minutes a day outlearns a two-hour cram session. Every lesson is sized to survive a busy week.

Mistakes are data

📈

Fluenta adapts around what you actually get wrong, not a fixed curriculum that ignores your level.

🌍

Native speakers, always

Every conversation room is hosted by a real native speaker — never a synthetic voice standing in for one.

How We Teach

A method built around real conversation

🎯

Placement, not a cold start

A short check-in places you on a path suited to what you already know, not a generic lesson one.

🧠

Spaced, adaptive review

Vocabulary resurfaces right before you'd naturally forget it, so it moves into long-term memory.

💬

Live rooms, not just recordings

Once you're ready, small-group conversation rooms turn recognition into actual spoken fluency.
