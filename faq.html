01 · Getting Started

Getting started

What to expect in your first week, from picking a language to setting a realistic goal.

Do I need any prior experience to start?
Not at all. Fluenta places you with a short placement check-in and builds a path from wherever you're starting, whether that's zero words or an old textbook's worth of rusty grammar.
How long does the placement check-in take?
About five minutes. It's a quick mix of listening and reading prompts, and you can retake it later if your level changes.
Can I set my own daily goal?
Yes. Choose a casual, steady, or intensive pace during onboarding, and adjust it anytime from your profile settings.
What happens if I miss a day?
Nothing punitive — your streak pauses and lessons pick up right where you left off. Streak freezes are also available on Pro and Teams plans.
02 · Lessons & Modes

Lessons & learning modes

How daily lessons adapt to you, and how Personal, Classroom, Teams, and Travel modes differ.

Can I switch or add languages later?
Yes. Free plan members can study one active language at a time, while Pro and Teams members can switch between or run multiple languages side by side with no extra cost.
How does Fluenta adapt lessons to my level?
Every answer you give — right, wrong, or hesitant — feeds a model of what you've mastered, so tomorrow's lesson leans harder on what still needs work.
What's the difference between Classroom and Teams mode?
Classroom is built for teachers pacing a curriculum with students; Teams is built for companies tracking fluency progress across employees with shared dashboards.
Is Travel mode a separate course?
No — it's a focused lesson set layered on top of your existing course, prioritizing survival phrases and audio for an upcoming trip.
03 · Conversation Rooms

Live conversation rooms

Everything about speaking live with native speakers, from booking a room to what happens if you're nervous.

How do the live conversation rooms work?
Rooms are small-group video sessions hosted by native speakers, matched to your level. You can join scheduled sessions or drop into an open room whenever one is running.
How many people are in a room at once?
Rooms are capped between four and six learners plus a host, so everyone gets real speaking time instead of watching from the sidelines.
What if I'm too nervous to speak?
Hosts are trained to ease first-timers in gently, and you can keep your camera off while you warm up. Most learners speak within the first ten minutes.
Are rooms included in the Free plan?
Free members can sit in on one room per month; Pro and Teams members get unlimited access to scheduled and open rooms.
04 · Billing & Plans

Billing & plans

Trials, upgrades, downgrades, and what happens to your progress if your plan changes.

Is there a free trial for the Pro plan?
Every new account gets a 14-day free trial of Pro, no card required upfront. You can downgrade to the Free plan anytime before it ends.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes, from your account settings with no phone call required. You'll keep Pro access until the end of your current billing period.
Do I lose my progress if I downgrade?
No. Your streak, XP, and lesson history stay intact — you'll just be limited to one active language until you upgrade again.
How does Teams pricing work?
Teams is priced per seat with volume discounts at scale. Reach out to sales for a quote based on your headcount and rollout timeline.
05 · Account & Technical

Account, privacy & technical

Syncing across devices, offline access, and how we handle your data.

Can I use Fluenta offline?
Pro and Teams members can download lessons for offline practice on mobile. Progress syncs automatically the next time you're back online.
Does my progress sync across devices?
Yes, instantly. Sign in with the same account on phone, tablet, or desktop and your streak, XP, and lesson position carry over.
Is my data GDPR compliant?
Yes. We follow GDPR data-handling standards, and you can request an export or deletion of your data from your account settings at any time.
How do I delete my account?
Go to Settings → Account → Delete account. This removes your profile and lesson history permanently, so we'll ask you to confirm once by email.
