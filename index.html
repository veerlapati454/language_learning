Four steps to fluency

No cramming, no guesswork. Fluenta breaks language learning into a simple daily loop that compounds over time. Drag or use the arrows to move through it.

STEP 01 / 04

Pick your language

Choose from 42 languages and set a fluency goal that fits your life and schedule.

STEP 02 / 04

Practice in minutes

Short, adaptive lessons meet you where you are — no filler, just steady daily reps.

STEP 03 / 04

Speak with real people

Join live conversation rooms and get natural feedback from native speakers.

STEP 04 / 04

Track your streak

Watch progress compound with streaks, XP, and milestones that keep you coming back.
