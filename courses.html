How Your Path Works

Every course follows the same three stages

1

Placement check-in

A short conversation-style check-in places you at the right level, so you never repeat what you already know.

2

Daily adaptive lessons

Ten to fifteen minutes a day, resequenced around exactly what you get wrong, not a fixed unit order.

3

Weekly live practice

Once your lessons unlock it, small-group conversation rooms turn recognition into real spoken fluency.

Course Levels

Structured from your first word to your first debate

Every language course is split into four levels, roughly mapped to the CEFR scale, so you always know what's next.

A1

Beginner

Greetings, everyday phrases, and enough grammar to build your first real sentences.

~40 hours
A2–B1

Intermediate

Hold a conversation about daily life, travel, and work without switching back to English.

~70 hours
B2

Advanced

Follow native-speed conversation, opinions, and media without needing things repeated.

~90 hours
C1

Fluent

Debate, negotiate, and joke in the language — the level most learners set out for.

~120 hours
