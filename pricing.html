Questions before you book

Can I switch plans later?
Yes — upgrade, downgrade, or switch between monthly and annual billing any time from your account settings. Changes to a higher plan apply immediately; changes to a lower plan apply at your next billing date.
What happens when I cancel?
You'll keep full access through the end of your current billing period, and your progress, streak, and saved words stay in your account if you come back. No cancellation fees, no retention calls.
Is there a student or family discount?
Verified students get 40% off Waypoint, and Resident supports up to 5 family members on one plan at a shared rate. Both are set up from the billing tab after you sign up.
Do unused live conversation rooms roll over?
Waypoint's monthly room credits reset each billing cycle and don't carry over, so we'd rather you use them than bank them. Resident includes unlimited rooms, so this isn't a concern on that plan.
Is my payment information secure?
All payments are processed through PCI-compliant providers — we never store your full card details on our servers. You'll see the padlock in your browser at checkout.
